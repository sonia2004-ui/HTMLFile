Chennai Companies

Chennai is a major IT and SaaS hub, housing massive global service enterprises and native product-based "unicorn" companies. While Zoho and Freshworks are renowned for their proprietary software products, TCS and Infosys are global giants delivering large-scale IT and consulting services.

Amazon India operates a massive tech hub in Bangalore (World Trade Center, Malleswaram) housing over 100,000 employees across technical and operational roles. Majority-owned by Walmart, its massive tech campus employs over 15,000 engineers and product professionals.

Hyderabad Companies

Hyderabad is one of India's premier IT hubs, hosting massive facilities for global tech leaders. Microsoft, Google, Deloitte, and Tech Mahindra all have major strategic campuses in the city, primarily focused on cloud engineering, AI, digital consulting, and enterprise IT services.

Hyderabad is a premier global tech hub, heavily concentrated in the HITEC City and Gachibowli corridors. Key players like Deloitte, Microsoft, and Tech Mahindra anchor the city's IT/ITES industry, employing tens of thousands of professionals across cloud engineering, IT consulting, and software development.

  • Google

    Hyderabad is home to Google's largest campus outside of the United States. Located in the Financial District (Gachibowli), this massive ~3.3 million sq. ft. facility is slated for completion in late 2026 and will house thousands of employees.

  • Deloitte

    Deloitte’s Hyderabad operations serve as one of its largest global hubs, housing a massive workforce across consulting, advisory, and technology sectors. Located primarily at the Deloitte Towers in the Meenakshi Tech Park (Gachibowli) and KRB Towers (Madhapur), it is a core operational center for Deloitte US-India (USI) Offices.

  • Microsoft

    Microsoft’s presence in Hyderabad is massive, housing the company's largest R&D hub outside its US headquarters. Located in the Financial District (Gachibowli), the smart, 54-acre campus employs thousands of engineers and professionals working on core global products like Azure, Bing, Office,AI initiative.

  • TechMahindra

    Tech Mahindra in Hyderabad is a major hub for the company's IT, engineering, and digital transformation services. Located primarily in Madhapur (Hi-Tech City) and Bahadurpally, the campus is a focal point for cutting-edge AI, ER&D (Engineering Research and Development), and consumer experience studios.
