A little bird finding its way

Once upon a time, a tiny blue bird lived in a tall green tree. He loved to sing songs, but his biggest dream was to fly across the sea. Every morning, he would watch the strong seagulls glide through the air. He always felt too small and scared to join them up there. One day, a gentle breeze whispered, "You will never know unless you try. "The brave little bird took a deep breath and opened his wings wide. He leaped from his cozy branch and gave a little flap. To his great surprise, he didn't fall into the gap. He caught the wind and soared higher than he had ever known

The bird's happiness

The beautiful blue sky felt completely like his very own. He flew over sparkling blue water and green, rolling hills. The feeling of freedom washed away all his little chills. He realized that believing in himself was all he really needed. From that day on, the happy bird was forever freed. Always remember to trust in your dreams and take that first leap!
