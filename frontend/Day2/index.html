Once upon a time, a young boy lived near a thick, green forest.Every day, he would walk to the woods to collect firewood to sell.One warm afternoon, he stumbled upon a tiny bird stuck in a bush.The poor little creature was fluttering its wings, unable to escape.The boy carefully untangled the branches and set the bird free.It chirped a happy tune and flew up into the tall trees. A few months later, the boy got lost in the very same forest. The sun was setting, and he did not know which way was home. Suddenly, a flock of birds flew down and circled around his head. They fluttered their wings and gently guided him along the path. He followed the birds until he safely reached his front door. The boy realized that the bird he saved had remembered him. He smiled, knowing that doing a good deed never goes to waste. Kindness is a magical gift that always comes back to you. The smallest act of help can make a huge difference in the end.
